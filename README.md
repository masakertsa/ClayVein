## 🔬 Scientific Grounding & Mechanics

This framework is built upon established geochemical and microbiological principles, scaled into a completely passive engineering architecture.

### 1. Geochemical Ion-Exchange & Clay Flocculation
The core relies on the high Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC) of localized mineral vectors:
- **Zeolite Matrices:** Utilize a three-dimensional crystalline aluminosilicate framework that acts as a molecular sieve [modified zeolite application](https://maxapress.com). It captures heavy metal cations (e.g., $Pb^{2+}$, $Cd^{2+}$) via ion-exchange, replacing them with harmless calcium or potassium ions [zeolite for toxic metal uptake](https://mdpi.com).
- **Pore Structural Shift:** High-density clay (such as kaolinite) has extremely low hydraulic conductivity. The introduction of the mineral core triggers localized flocculation, altering the soil pH and aggregate stability [effect of biochar, zeolite and bentonite](https://sciencedirect.com). This opens the dense clay matrix, establishing a permanent capillary gradient toward the shaft.

### 2. Cyanobacterial Biosorption & Metabolic Bioaccumulation
The surface blanket leverages the extreme resilience of photosynthetic cyanobacteria (specifically *Nostoc* strains):
- **Passive Biosorption:** The extracellular polymeric substances (EPS) and cell walls of cyanobacteria contain negatively charged functional groups (carboxyl, hydroxyl, and phosphoryl). These act as natural chemical magnets, binding upward-migrating heavy metals with a verified removal efficiency of up to 91–98% [bioaccumulation and biosorption study](https://researchgate.net).
- **Active Bioaccumulation:** Toxins that cross the cellular membrane are actively translocated into intracellular vacuoles. The matrix survives severe chemical stress by upregulating antioxidant enzymes, allowing continuous solar-driven metabolic pumping without biomass degradation.
