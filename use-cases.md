# 🗺️ ClayVein Field Implementations: Three Core Use Cases

The ClayVein framework scales dynamically based on the specific type of contamination. Below are the three standard recipes for field deployment, optimizing the mineral core ratio ($Zeolite : Bentonite$) and the grid spacing ($S$).

---

## 🪨 Scenario A: Heavy Metal Lockdown (Pb, Cd, Cu)
*Designed for soils contaminated by old industrial activity, paints, or urban runoff.*

*   **Mineral Core Ratio:** **70% Zeolite / 30% Bentonite**
    *   *Logic:* High zeolite content maximizes the cation-exchange capacity (CEC) to capture heavy metal ions ($Pb^{2+}$, $Cd^{2+}$), while bentonite acts as a structural binder.
*   **Grid Spacing ($S$):**
    *   Dense Clay: **40–50 cm** intervals.
    *   Porous/Sandy Soil: **60–80 cm** intervals.
*   **Biological Action:** *Nostoc* cyanobacteria cap absorbs the upward capillary toxic plume with up to 91–98% biosorption efficiency. The heavy metals accumulate in the surface crust, which is skimmed off at the end of the season.

---

## 🛢️ Scenario B: Hydrocarbon Sponge (Fuel Oil, Diesel, Grease)
*Designed for vehicle maintenance areas, old tank leaks, or machine shops.*

*   **Mineral Core Ratio:** **30% Zeolite / 70% Bentonite** *(or pure organo-bentonite)*
    *   *Logic:* Hydrocarbons are hydrophobic and do not dissolve in water. High bentonite concentrations expand upon hydration, creating a physical "sponge" that traps organic hydrocarbon molecules.
*   **Grid Spacing ($S$):**
    *   All Soil Types: **20–30 cm** (Very Dense Grid).
    *   *Logic:* Because oil does not travel easily via capillary action, the macro-puncture network must be dense enough so the contamination plume directly intersects the mineral cores.
*   **Biological Action:** Cyanobacterial Extracellular Polymeric Substances (EPS) neutralize volatile chemical vapors reaching the surface.

---

## 🌾 Scenario C: Fertilizer & Nutrient Recovery (Excess Nitrogen & Phosphate)
*Designed for agricultural runoff zones, over-fertilized fields, or eutrophied soil.*

*   **Mineral Core Ratio:** **80% Zeolite / 20% Bentonite**
    *   *Logic:* Zeolite has an extremely high affinity for ammonium-nitrogen ($NH_4^+$), locking it in place and preventing it from leaching into the groundwater.
*   **Grid Spacing ($S$):**
    *   Dense Clay: **60–80 cm** intervals.
    *   Porous/Sandy Soil: **80–120 cm** intervals.
    *   *Logic:* Nutrients dissolve fully and migrate rapidly with pore-water, allowing a wider, more economical grid setup.
*   **Biological Action:** The *Nostoc* matrix utilizes the locked-in nitrogen and phosphorus as a fuel source, exploding in biomass. This nutrient-rich biomass can later be harvested and recycled as clean, high-grade organic compost elsewhere.
